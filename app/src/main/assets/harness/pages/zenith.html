Zephyr Lantern Museum

The Zephyr Lantern Museum is a small museum of lighting history.

Visiting hours: 9am to 5pm, every day of the week.

The collection covers oil lamps, gas lights and early electric lanterns.

Guided tours run at noon and at three in the afternoon.

The museum shop sells postcards, candle kits and small lanterns.

Admission is free for children under twelve years of age.

The reading room holds the museum's archive of lighting catalogues.

Group visits can be arranged by writing to the museum office.

Every winter the museum hosts a lantern parade through the old town.

Volunteers restore the collection's lamps in the open workshop.

The museum cafe serves tea, coffee and homemade cakes.

Photography is allowed in all galleries without flash.

The east wing reopens next spring after renovation work.

Accessibility: step-free access to every gallery and the cafe.

How to find us: two streets from the harbour, past the old customs house.

The museum was founded by the Zephyr Lighting Company in 1952.

Opening hours on holidays may differ; check the notice board.

Thank you for visiting the Zephyr Lantern Museum.
